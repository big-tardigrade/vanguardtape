Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really ship to Malaysia?
Yes — tracked door-to-door air freight from our factory in Tangerang. If you'd rather see it work first, order a sample pack.
Who pays the import duty and customs tax?
We do. Duties, taxes and clearance are handled on our side and already included in your price. If customs charges something unexpected, we absorb it. No second bill at your door.
How long does it really take?
Under 3 weeks — and that clock starts when your design is approved and the deposit is paid, not from your first message. It's 5–9 working days production, then 6–7 days air freight to West Malaysia or 8–10 days to Sabah and Sarawak. So roughly 11–16 days to the Peninsular and 13–19 days to East Malaysia. Quoting and design beforehand run at your pace. Slow customs can add a few days; if that happens we'll tell you rather than go quiet.
What's the minimum order?
One carton — 72 rolls at 48mm, up to 288 rolls at 12mm. Most Malaysian orders are 1 to 3 cartons and that's completely normal for us.
Can I pay in Ringgit?
We quote in Ringgit so you know exactly what you're spending, and invoice in Rupiah at the agreed rate. Remittance app, Touch 'n Go, Wise or SWIFT transfer all work. Details come on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.
Do I have to pay everything upfront?
No. 30% deposit to start, 70% after production — and you see video and photos of your finished rolls before paying that 70%. Paying in full upfront is optional, never required.
Is the RM price on the table my final price?
It's our real factory price at the live rate, not a teaser — but the exchange rate moves daily and freight depends on final weight. Edit the rate box to match your bank. Your final figure is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay anything.
What if the print comes out wrong?
Minor colour variation where the tape still works: direct compensation. Anything major — wrong product, unusable print, broken goods: full free reprint. Transit damage: we claim against the courier ourselves.
Can I get a sample first?
Yes, about RM 23, roughly our freight cost, around 10 days. Note that samples are existing printed rolls in similar colours, not your own design — they're for judging film, adhesive and print quality. Your artwork is approved through the free mockup.
I don't have a designer. Can you still do it?
That's the normal situation. Send a logo file, a screenshot, even a photo of your signage. We lay it out for tape and send a mockup, free, with revisions until you approve. No design, plate or setup fee, ever.
Will the adhesive hold in Malaysian weather?
Yes — Indonesia has the same climate, and this is what we produce and use every day. Like any tape, don't store rolls in direct sunlight or a hot vehicle; kept in normal warehouse conditions it performs exactly as expected. Heavy cartons? Ask us about 50 micron.
Which parts of Malaysia do you deliver to?
Nationwide — Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru, Ipoh, Melaka, Kota Kinabalu, Kuching and beyond. West Malaysia is quicker and cheaper to reach; Sabah and Sarawak take a few days longer and cost more to ship. Somewhere remote? Send us the postcode and we'll confirm transit time first.
Why order from Indonesia?
Speed, price and access. We're a neighbour, not a distant supplier — affordable air freight puts your tape here in under 3 weeks, our factory pricing is competitive, and one carton is a real order. Same time zone too, so you're not waiting overnight for a reply.
How do I know you're real?
Our factory address is on this page and on Google Maps. You'll deal with Justin, our manager, directly on WhatsApp — not a bot. Ask for photos or a video of the line and you'll get them. And you never pay in full before seeing your finished product.
